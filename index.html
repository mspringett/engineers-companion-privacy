Engineers Companion Privacy Policy

Engineers Companion is provided by Tango47. For support or privacy questions, contact Mspringett@yahoo.com.

Calculations and information on your device

Calculations run on your device. App preferences, favorite calculators, and document settings are stored locally. Reference documents you download are also stored on your device. The app does not provide a user account or cloud synchronization. The AI preference setting does not connect an AI service or send your information to one.

Optional feedback

If you send feedback, we receive the category and message you enter, including any reproduction steps, along with the app version and platform. The server adds a record identifier and time. We use this information to review reports and suggestions and improve the app. Please do not include passwords, confidential work information, or sensitive personal information.

Feedback is processed and stored using Supabase. An anti-abuse system also stores a fingerprint of the message/category and receipt time to limit duplicate and excessive submissions. Expired anti-abuse receipts are removed during subsequent requests; feedback messages are not automatically removed after that period.

Connections and other services

Online requests use HTTPS. Servers handling these requests receive connection information, such as an IP address, and may retain operational or security logs. The app does not add an advertising identifier or device identifier to feedback.

Downloading reference documents contacts the document publisher. Opening an external reference or the optional support link takes you to another service. Those services handle information under their own privacy policies. PayPal processes optional contributions; the app does not collect your payment-card details. Contributions do not unlock app features.

Storage and your choices

Sending feedback is optional. Current feedback storage has no automatic expiry: messages remain until the developer removes them. Contact the address above about stored feedback, including enough detail to identify the submission. No user account or submission identifier is returned by the app, so locating a message may require its approximate date and text.

You can remove downloaded documents using the available app controls. Clearing the app's storage removes its local preferences and downloaded files. This does not remove feedback already sent to the server or copies managed by other services or device backups. Provider logs and backups follow their separate retention processes.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated when the app's data practices change. Send questions or requests to Mspringett@yahoo.com.
