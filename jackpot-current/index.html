Jackpot Current Test Privacy Policy

Jackpot Current Test is provided by Tango 47. For support or privacy questions, contact support@formaexideis.com.

This policy describes the invitation-only Android test version of the app. Power Chips are virtual, have no cash value, and cannot be redeemed for money or prizes. This version has no purchases.

Information on your device

The app stores game progress, Power Chip balance, Power Gems, rewards, and game settings locally on your device. It also stores music, sound, haptics, display, notification, and analytics preferences locally. Your keep-screen-on setting is stored in Android app preferences. This test version has no game account or server sync.

Optional usage analytics

Usage analytics are off until you choose Share analytics in the app or enable Share anonymous usage analytics in Settings. If you previously enabled the narrower gameplay analytics, the app asks for your agreement to this expanded scope before sending new events. A previous decline remains in effect unless you change it in Settings.

When enabled, the app sends a random app-generated installation identifier, a random session identifier, the app version, timestamps, the area viewed (Lobby, Slots, Keno, or Video Poker), and time spent there to the separate Jackpot Current Supabase project. It also records when you open the simulated chip store or Piggy Bank, select a test offer, or complete a free test unlock. These events may include a fixed offer name and random test-flow and event identifiers. After a test unlock, you may optionally answer whether you would consider buying the displayed offer at its displayed price. Only your choice of Yes, Maybe, or No is sent; Skip sends no answer. Tango 47 uses this information to understand play time and interest in test offers.

Test unlocks are free; they are not real purchases. Analytics events do not include your name, email address, advertising identifier, device hardware identifier, location, contacts, Power Chip balance, bet amount, win or loss, jackpot result, real payment information, scratch-ticket prize, or other game outcome. You may turn analytics off in Settings. Turning it off stops future collection and clears unsent events from the device. Contact the privacy address below to request deletion of previously received analytics associated with the random installation identifier, when that identifier can be supplied.

Sharing and other services

This version has no advertising or in-app purchases. If you opt in to anonymous usage analytics, the limited events described above are processed by Supabase on Tango 47's behalf. If you opt in to the daily scratch reminder, Android schedules it on your device; no remote push-notification service receives your gameplay data. The app includes its artwork and audio locally. If you open a link in Credits, Android opens a third-party website in your browser; that website handles information under its own privacy policy. Google Play separately handles app delivery and related diagnostics under Google's policies.

Your choices and deletion

You may change sound, music, haptics, display, daily-reminder, and analytics settings in the app. Reset prototype progress clears the balance, game progress, rewards, and Power Gems while retaining preferences. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes its active local records. Device or service backups may retain copies according to their own settings. There is no online game account to delete in this version.

Changes and contact

If a later version adds accounts, remote push notifications, purchases, advertising, or additional data collection, this policy and the Google Play Data safety answers will be updated before that version is distributed. Questions about this version can be sent to support@formaexideis.com.
