Jackpot Current Test Privacy Policy

Jackpot Current Test is provided by Tango 47. For support or privacy questions, contact support@formaexideis.com.

This policy describes the invitation-only Android test version of the app. Power Chips are virtual, have no cash value, and cannot be redeemed for money or prizes. This version has no purchases.

Information on your device

The app stores game progress, Power Chip balance, Power Gems, rewards, and game settings locally on your device. It also stores music, sound, haptics, and display preferences locally. Your keep-screen-on setting is stored in Android app preferences. The app has no account or game-server sync, and this test version does not send gameplay records to Tango 47.

Sharing and other services

This version has no advertising, analytics, push notifications, or in-app purchases. It includes its artwork and audio locally. If you open a link in Credits, Android opens a third-party website in your browser; that website handles information under its own privacy policy. Google Play separately handles app delivery and related diagnostics under Google's policies.

Storage and your choices

Game progress and preferences remain on your device until you change or remove them. Reset prototype progress clears the balance, game progress, rewards, and Power Gems while retaining preferences. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes its active local records. Device or service backups may retain copies according to their own settings. There is no online game account to delete in this version.

Changes and contact

If a later version adds accounts, analytics, notifications, purchases, or other data collection, this policy will be updated before that version is distributed. Questions about this version can be sent to support@formaexideis.com.
